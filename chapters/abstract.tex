This report presents an end-to-end pipeline to measure and optimize neural network inference energy on edge devices. It connects three modular tools:

\textbf{JouleQuest} profiles AI workloads on embedded platforms (Jetson~Nano, Jetson AGX~Orin, Raspberry~Pi~5) using an INA226 power sensor. It automates calibration, clock synchronization, and burst sizing to build physical energy lookup tables for isolated neural network layers.

\textbf{JouleGrad} turns these empirical tables into differentiable energy estimates, making hardware cost a direct regularization penalty during training.

\textbf{JouleNAS} uses these estimates to guide channel pruning on frozen vision backbones (SimpleConv and ResNet-18 on CIFAR-10 and Imagenette), discovering architectures tailored to each board's real physical energy costs.

Finally, this report serves as a practical handbook for future researchers to reproduce the experiments, extend the ecosystem, and profile new embedded hardware.
